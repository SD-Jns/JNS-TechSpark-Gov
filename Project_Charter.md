# AI Government Project Tracker
## Project Charter

---

## Project Overview

The AI Government Project Tracker is a centralized platform designed to improve the monitoring, reporting, and transparency of government development projects. The solution leverages Artificial Intelligence to provide project insights, identify risks, summarize progress updates, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Purpose

Government projects often involve multiple stakeholders, long implementation periods, and frequent reporting requirements. Project information is commonly distributed across spreadsheets, reports, emails, and individual systems, making it difficult to track progress effectively.

The purpose of this project is to create a centralized AI-powered platform that enables stakeholders to monitor project status, track milestones, identify delays, and generate real-time insights.

---

## Problem Statement

Government development projects are often tracked using fragmented and manual processes. This results in:

- Limited visibility of project progress
- Delayed identification of risks and bottlenecks
- Inefficient reporting processes
- Poor coordination among stakeholders
- Reduced transparency and accountability

An intelligent project tracking system is required to provide timely, accurate, and actionable project information.
